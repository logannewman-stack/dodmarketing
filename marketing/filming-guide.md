# What to film, and how

You don't need a film crew. You need the same 12 shots from every job, filmed the same way. After two or three weeks of jobs you'll have enough footage to cut every ad in [facebook-ads.md](facebook-ads.md) and keep making new ones.

---

## Gear

| What | Why | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|
| Your phone (iPhone 12 or newer, or a recent Android) | It's all the camera you need | You have it |
| Small tripod with a phone clamp | Matching before/after shots, time-lapses | $20–30 |
| Wireless lav mic (DJI Mic, Rode Wireless, or a Hollyland clone) | Talking-to-camera ads. Phone mics outdoors pick up wind and traffic | $50–150 |
| Microfiber cloth for the lens | A smudged lens is the most common reason footage looks cheap | $0 |
| Cheap LED flashlight | Shows swirl marks for paint correction shots | $15 |

## Phone settings (once)

- **Vertical**, always. You can crop vertical down to 4:5; you can't turn horizontal into a Reel.
- **4K at 30 fps** for normal shots. Use **1080p at 60 or 120 fps** for slow-mo (foam, polisher, water sheeting).
- **Lock focus and exposure:** press and hold on the subject until "AE/AF Lock" appears. Otherwise the phone refocuses mid-shot.
- **Grid on**, to keep horizons level and match your before/after framing.
- **Wipe the lens** before every job.

## Light

- Overcast days and the first or last hour of sun look best. Midday sun blows out white paint and hides dirt.
- For interiors, open every door and shoot with the light behind you.
- For paint, put the sun or your flashlight at an angle across the panel so swirls show up.

## The 12-shot checklist (every job)

Film all 12 on every job, even the boring ones. Each shot takes 5–10 seconds; the whole list adds about 10 minutes to a job. Name the clips or put them in a phone album per job.

| # | Shot | How to film it | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Before, wide** | Full car from the front corner, 6 ft back, tripod. **Mark where the tripod stood** (chalk, or a photo of the spot) | A1, A4, S1 |
| 2 | **Worst spots, close-up** | 3–4 clips: seat seams, under the seats, cupholders, floor mats, pet hair. Slow push-in | Hooks for A1, A5 |
| 3 | **Arrival** | Your vehicle pulling into their driveway, filmed by a helper or propped on the dash | A2, A4, P1 |
| 4 | **Foam** | Foam cannon covering the car, wide and then close, slow-mo | A4, A7, B-roll |
| 5 | **The extraction stripe** | One slow, continuous pass of the extractor across a dirty seat or carpet, with the phone locked on a tripod or held very still. **The single most important shot** | A1, A5 |
| 6 | **The dirty water** | Pour the extractor tank into a clear cup or jar, held up to the light | A1 |
| 7 | **Detail work** | 1–2 second clips: brush in a vent, swab in a cupholder, a wheel brush in the spokes, a glass wipe | A1, A4, A5 |
| 8 | **Reveal wipes** | One wipe that takes dirt off in a single pass: a door jamb, a dash, a wheel | Hooks, transitions |
| 9 | **50/50** (paint jobs) | Tape a line down a panel. Flashlight on the swirls, polish one side, flashlight again, peel the tape | A7 |
| 10 | **After, wide** | **The exact same spot and framing as shot 1.** This is what makes a before/after work | Every ad |
| 11 | **After, details** | Clean seats, shiny wheels, a reflection in the paint, water beading off ceramic | Every ad |
| 12 | **Customer reaction** | Customer opens the door and sees it. Don't script it. Just ask "What do you think?" | A2, testimonials |

**Bonus shot (once):** a screen recording of booking in the customer app, from address to confirmation. On an iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording. You need it for A2, A3 and R2.

### Matching before and after

Before/after only works when the two shots line up exactly. Same tripod spot, same height, same zoom, same doors open. Take a photo of the tripod spot during the "before" so you can find it again three hours later.

## Customer permission

Ask before you film, every time:

> "Mind if I film a bit of this for our social media? I won't show your face, your plate or your house number unless you're OK with it."

- If they say yes to appearing, get it in writing (a text that says "Yes, you can use video of me and my car in Detail On Demand ads" is enough).
- Blur license plates and house numbers in CapCut (Effects → Blur, or the Mosaic sticker).
- Offer $20 off their next detail if they'll do a 10-second reaction on camera. Real customers on camera outperform everything else.

## Recording voiceovers

1. **Where:** a closet full of clothes, or inside your car with the windows up. Soft surfaces stop echo.
2. **How:** phone Voice Memos (or CapCut's Record button), phone 6 inches from your mouth, slightly off to the side so your breath doesn't pop.
3. **Delivery:**
   - Stand up. Your voice has more energy standing.
   - Smile while you talk. You can hear it.
   - Talk like you're texting a friend a voice note, not reading an ad.
   - Say the numbers the way people say them: "a hundred ninety-nine", not "one-nine-nine dollars".
   - Record **3 takes** of every line and keep the best one.
4. **Speed:** about 150 words a minute. The scripts are written to fit their lengths at that pace.
5. **Hooks:** record every hook option separately so you can swap them in the edit.

If you'd rather not voice every ad, have the customer voice A2 and a pro voice P2, and keep your voice for A1, A3 and R1. People trust the founder.

## Editing in CapCut (free)

1. **New project**, 9:16.
2. **Voiceover first:** drop in your voiceover, then lay clips over it. Cut a new clip every 1–2 seconds; nothing sits still for longer than 3.
3. **Captions:** Text → Auto captions. Then:
   - Font: bold sans-serif, white with a black outline or a black box.
   - Size: large, 2–4 words per line.
   - Position: middle of the screen, between 40% and 60% of the height.
4. **Hook text:** big, top-middle (below the top 15%), on screen for the first 2–3 seconds.
5. **Music:** low, under the voice. Use tracks marked **commercial use** (CapCut's commercial library, or Meta's Sound Collection). A popular song in a paid ad can get the ad pulled.
6. **End card** (last 3 seconds): logo on navy (`#040D1F`), "Full detail from $199 · Pay after it's done", and "Tap Book Now". Build it once and reuse it.
7. **Export:** 1080p, 30 fps. Then duplicate the project, swap the first 2 seconds for hook #2 and export, then again for hook #3.
8. **4:5 version:** duplicate the project, change the canvas to 4:5 and reposition.

### Brand look for end cards and text

- Navy `#040D1F` background, blue `#0A84FF` for buttons and accents, light blue `#60A5FA` for highlight words.
- Headlines in a tall, bold, all-caps font (Oswald, or CapCut's closest match).
- Logo in white on navy.

## First two weeks: filming plan

| Day | Film |
|---|---|
| Every job | The 12-shot checklist |
| Week 1, any day | A3 founder video (one 20-minute session, with 3 hook takes) |
| Week 1, any day | App screen recording |
| First messy interior | Everything A1 and A5 need |
| First paint correction | Shot 9 (50/50) for A7 |
| Any customer who's up for it | A2 couch video (offer them $20 off) |
| One of your pros | P2 day-in-the-life, filmed on their phone |

By the end of week 2 you should have A1, A2, A3, A4 and R1 live, each with 3 hooks.
