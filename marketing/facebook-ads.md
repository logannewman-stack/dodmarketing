# Detail On Demand: Facebook & Instagram ad scripts

Every ad here is written to be filmed on a phone, voiced by you (or a customer), and edited in CapCut. How to shoot and record is in [filming-guide.md](filming-guide.md). How the clicks turn into bookings is in [signup-funnel.md](signup-funnel.md).

Replace `[CITY]` with the market you're running in. Say it out loud in the voiceover and put it in the first line of the caption. Local ads that name the city get noticeably more clicks than generic ones.

---

## The offer (use it everywhere)

Every customer ad makes the same three promises. Repeating one clear offer beats a different pitch in every ad.

1. **Full detail, inside and out, from $199.** This is the Full Detail price in the app's checkout for a car. It's $229 for an SUV, $239 for a truck and $259 for a 3-row.
2. **We come to you.** Home, office or apartment lot.
3. **You don't pay until it's done.** The app authorizes the card at booking and charges it after the job is complete. Before launch, fix the Terms and the confirmation screen that still mention a 25% deposit. See [fix-before-launch.md](fix-before-launch.md).

For retargeting, add **DETAIL10 (10% off)**. It's the only promo code the app's checkout accepts right now.

### Don't say these yet

These claims are in the old app or site copy, but the product doesn't back them up. An ad that promises one of them gets you refund requests and bad reviews.

| Don't say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Track your detailer in real time" | There's no live GPS. Customers get status updates and chat. |
| "Nationwide" | Scheduling only matches a pro within 15 miles. Only advertise where you have pros. |
| "Give $20, get $20" / memberships / WEEKEND15 | None of these can be redeemed in checkout today. |
| "5,000+ cars", "4.9 stars from 2.1k reviews" | These numbers are hard-coded and conflict with each other. Use your real Google numbers. |
| "Background-checked and insured" | Confirm this is true for every active pro first. The app doesn't collect insurance proof. |
| "We bring our own water and power" | Only true if every pro carries a tank and a generator. |

## How every video is built

The first 2 seconds decide whether anyone watches. Each script gives you three hooks. Film the body of the ad once, then export three versions that differ only in the first 2 seconds. That's the cheapest test you can run.

| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0–2s | **Hook.** The grossest or most satisfying shot, with bold on-screen text and the first spoken line. No logo, no intro. |
| 2–12s | **Proof.** Before → process → after. Show the transformation instead of describing it. |
| 12–20s | **Offer.** $199, we come to you, pay after. Say `[CITY]`. |
| Last 3s | **Call to action.** "Tap Book Now to get your price." Put the logo on the end card. |

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920. Also export a 4:5 crop (1080×1350) for the Feed.
- **Length:** 15–30 seconds for cold audiences. Founder and testimonial ads can run 30–45 seconds.
- **Captions:** burned in, every word. Most people watch muted.
- **Safe zone:** Facebook covers the top ~15% and bottom ~35% of a Reel with buttons and caption. Keep text in the middle.

# Customer ads

## A1. "Watch the water" (interior before/after)

The best-performing format in detailing: disgusting to spotless in 20 seconds. Make this one first.

**Film on:** the dirtiest interior you get this month. Ask the customer first (see the filming guide).
**Length:** 20–24s. **Voice:** you.

**Hooks. Pick three and export three versions:**
1. On screen: `3 years of school runs.` Say: "This is what three years of school runs does to a car."
2. On screen: `Watch the water.` Say nothing for one second over the extractor shot, then start the voiceover.
3. On screen: `Rate this interior 1–10` Say: "Be honest, what would you rate this interior?"

| Time | Shot | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Extreme close-up of the worst spot (seat seam full of crumbs, stained seat, dog hair), fast push-in | Hook text |
| 2–5s | Extractor pulling a clean stripe through a dirty seat, in one continuous pass | — |
| 5–7s | The dirty water: extractor tank or a clear cup of it held to the camera | `😬` |
| 7–11s | Three 1-second cuts: brush in a vent, wiping a cupholder, pressure-washing a floor mat | — |
| 11–15s | **After:** exact same angle as 0–2s, slow push-in | `After` |
| 15–19s | Wide shot: the car in a driveway with doors open, house behind it | `We come to you` |
| 19–23s | End card: logo, and "Full detail from $199 · Pay after it's done" | `Tap Book Now` |

**Voiceover** (about 55 words):
> This is what three years of school runs does to a car.
> And this is the same car after one visit from Detail On Demand.
> We come to your driveway, and you don't pay until it's done.
> Full detail, inside and out, from a hundred ninety-nine in [CITY].
> Tap Book Now and get your price in thirty seconds.

**Caption (primary text):**
```
[CITY], your car detailed in your driveway.

✅ Full detail inside & out from $199
✅ A vetted pro comes to your home or office
✅ Your card isn't charged until the job's done

Tap "Book Now" to get your price in 30 seconds.
```
**Headline:** Full Detail From $199. We Come to You.
**Description:** Pay after it's done
**Button:** Book Now

## A3. Founder story (Logan to camera)

This ad builds trust. It won't be the cheapest per click, but people who watch it book at higher rates, and it's the best video to retarget with.

**Film on:** you, standing next to a car mid-detail in a real driveway. Hold the phone at eye level, or have someone film you. Use a lav mic.
**Length:** 35–40s.

**Hooks:**
1. "If you haven't cleaned your car since summer, this is for you."
2. "Here's why most people in [CITY] never get their car detailed."
3. "I'm Logan, and I'll detail your car without you leaving the house."

| Time | Shot |
|---|---|
| 0–3s | You to camera, mid-motion (wiping down a panel), then turn to the lens |
| 3–12s | You talking, with B-roll cut in every 3–4 seconds: foam, extraction, wheel brush |
| 12–25s | Screen recording of the booking flow while you keep talking over it |
| 25–33s | Before/after pair from a real job |
| 33–38s | Back to you, pointing down: "Tap below" |

> If you haven't cleaned your car since summer, this is for you.

> Full detail, inside and out, starts at a hundred ninety-nine.
> [CITY], tap below and get your price.

```
I started Detail On Demand because nobody has a spare Saturday to sit at a detail shop.

Here's how it works in [CITY]:
1️⃣ Book on your phone in about a minute
2️⃣ A Detail Pro comes to your home or office
```

**Length:** 20s. **Voice:** you, calm and technical.

# Image ads

These are cheap to make, and good for the Feed and for testing prices.

**S1. Before/after split** (1080×1350). Top half: `BEFORE` over the worst photo. Bottom half: `AFTER` from the same angle. Band across the middle: `Full detail in your driveway · from $199`.

**S2. Price carousel** (1080×1080 cards):
1. Photo of a pro in a driveway: `Your car. Your driveway.`

# Detail Pro recruiting ads

These send detailers to `https://doddetailer.com/join` (waitlist) or `/apply` (full application). The funnel doc explains which to use where.

Don't put weekly income figures in these ads. Earnings claims in recruiting ads draw FTC scrutiny, and the numbers in the app are demo data. Stick to the split and the facts below.

## P1. "Stop chasing clients"

**Length:** 25s. **Voice:** you, detailer to detailer.

**Hooks:**
1. On screen: `Detailers: how many hours a week do you spend finding customers?`
2. On screen: `You're a detailer, not a marketer.`
3. On screen: `[CITY] detailers, we have customers waiting.` (Only if true.)

**Voiceover** (about 70 words):
> Detailers in [CITY], how much of your week goes to finding customers instead of detailing?
> Detail On Demand brings them to you. Customers book and pay in our app, jobs get sent to pros nearby, and you take the ones you want.
> You keep forty percent of every job, a hundred percent of your tips, and you set your own hours.
> Apply in about five minutes at the link.

**Shots:** your phone showing an incoming job request in Detail Pro, a pro pulling into a driveway, quick detailing cuts, the Detail Pro earnings screen (blur the real numbers), a tip notification.
**Headline:** Get Detailing Customers Sent to You
**Button:** Apply Now

Confirm first: before any ad says "paid weekly", make sure payouts really go out weekly. The earnings screen currently says payouts are sent manually. See the fix list.

## P2. "A day as a Detail Pro" (POV)

**Length:** 30s, filmed by one of your pros on their phone.
> 7am. Phone buzzes, full detail, 4 miles away. Accept.

> Customer tips twenty bucks. That's all mine.
> Two more jobs this afternoon, all booked for me.
> If you detail in [CITY], this could be your day. Link below.

Only have your pro say numbers that happened on the day you filmed.

**Length:** 20s.
> Want to start detailing but don't have the equipment?
> DOD Setups builds a complete mobile detailing rig for your vehicle: water tank, pressure washer, generator, vacuum and chemicals, from eighteen forty-nine.

## Naming your ads

Name each ad `A1-hook2-CITY`. The landing page records the ad name with every lead (as `utm_content`), so you can see which ad and hook brought in each customer, not just each click. The URL setup is in [signup-funnel.md](signup-funnel.md#tracking).
