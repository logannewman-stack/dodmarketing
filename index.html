Questions

Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as your pro can reach the car and has a safe place to work, you can get on with your day. Plenty of people book while they're at the office.

Do you need my water or power?

Usually not. Pros bring their own equipment and products. If your pro needs a hose or an outlet for your location, they'll tell you when they confirm.

How does payment work?

Your card is authorized when you book and only charged after the detail is done. You can add a tip in the app afterward, and 100% of it goes to your pro.

Which areas do you cover?

Enter your ZIP in the form. We'll confirm there's a pro near you before anything is booked, so you're never waiting on a detail that can't happen.

How long does it take?

About 2 hours for an interior detail, 3 for a full detail, 5 for paint correction and a full day for ceramic coating. Bigger or dirtier vehicles can take longer.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule for free up to 24 hours before your appointment.

Is there an app?

Yes. Detail On Demand is on the iPhone App Store. Fill in the form above and we'll text you a booking link whichever phone you have.
