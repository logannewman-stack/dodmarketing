Before you book

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as your pro can reach the car and has a safe place to work, you can get on with your day.

What if my time isn't available?

Your pro confirms by text within the hour. If that exact slot is taken, you pick another from what's open. Your card isn't charged until the job is done, so a change costs nothing.

Do you need my water or power?

Usually not. Pros bring their own equipment and products. If your pro needs a hose or an outlet at your location, they'll tell you when they confirm.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel or reschedule free up to 24 hours before your appointment. The hold on your card is released.

Is there an app?

Yes. Detail On Demand is on the iPhone App Store. Once you're booked, you can track the job, message your pro and rebook from the app.
