Privacy policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

This policy covers the booking request form on this page. Bookings made in the Detail On Demand app are also covered by the app's own terms and privacy policy.

What we collect

  • What you type into the form: your name, mobile number, the ZIP code where the car is, the service you want and your vehicle size.
  • Where you came from, such as the ad or link you tapped (UTM tags and Facebook click ID).
  • If you've allowed it in your browser, Meta Pixel data about your visit, used to measure and improve our ads.

How we use it

  • To text or call you with a price and open times for the detail you asked about.
  • To match your request with a Detail Pro near you. Your pro sees what they need to do the job.
  • To send occasional offers by text, only if you ticked the optional box. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.
  • To see which ads bring in bookings.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that run this form, our texting and our ads, and with the Detail Pro who takes your job.

Texts

Submitting the form means you agree to get texts about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Your choices
